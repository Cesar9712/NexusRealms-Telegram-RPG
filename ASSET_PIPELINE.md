# Asset Pipeline

Art direction: original dark fantasy + modern anime RPG, obsidian/charcoal with gold, bronze, dark red, arcane violet and blue. Do not copy Nexus Realms proprietary art.

Target mobile formats: AVIF/WebP for backgrounds/portraits, compressed SVG for UI icons where appropriate, spritesheets for small effects, OGG/AAC audio fallbacks. Provide low/medium/high effect tiers and lazy-load noncritical art.

Production folders live under `apps/miniapp/public/assets/`: characters, enemies, bosses, items, weapons, armor, maps, bastion, buildings, skills, effects, icons, ui, backgrounds, pets, mounts, events and audio. Current CSS/SVG procedural art is a professional temporary layer until final asset packs are approved.
