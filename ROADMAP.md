# Roadmap

## Foundation — implemented
Telegram auth, bot/Mini App/API/worker split, PostgreSQL model, character creation, realm persistence, combat engine, core UI, CI.

## Gameplay — implemented baseline
50+ enemies, 12 bosses, skills, quests, crafting/professions, Bastion, inventory, achievements, Codex, Battle Pass, referrals, daily rewards, events, clans/raids, internal market, pets/mounts, Arena and admin APIs.

## Production hardening
Real-device QA, final art/audio packs, balance telemetry, accessibility, localization completion, load testing and live-content tooling.

## Later feature flags
Clan wars/territory seasons. Web3 or withdrawals remain outside launch scope and disabled until separately authorized and compliant infrastructure exists.
